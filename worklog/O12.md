Woodpecker box (median 6.6 to 17.4 ms; 2.1 ms alone). The test is unchanged. Reworked after review
6008872330 (the first version made integration wait behind the hours-long jobs and failed NRV-E1245).
`ci/woodpecker_step.sh` now runs the job inside a host-wide reader/writer lock (`ci_box_lock`,
`ci_box_unlock`, `ci/cache.sh`) around the run alone: short jobs `locks/box.lock` shared, `integration`
builds shared (`ci/job.sh integration-build`) then runs its test exclusive (released and re-taken in
turnstile, box order, never waiting while holding shared), with `locks/turnstile.lock` against
starvation. The long jobs (`fuzz-long`, `vopr-long`, `jepsen`) take no lock; integration may still fail on
timing beside them (accepted, O12). `CI_BOX_WAIT` 4800 s, `CI_INTEGRATION_RUN_MINUTES` 15, NRV-E1245
(message names the holder) and NRV-B1203 (docs/ERRORS.md). `ci/ci_test.sh`: real flock tests, a
killed holder frees the lock, fds 9 to 11 closed for the job and the root tests, the order of the step.
Not run on the real box. Decision O12.
