# PR 12 — 2026-11-22

NervaOrg/nerva#12 (fixes #11): `test:integration`'s one-tick lookup bound failed on the loaded
Woodpecker box (median 6.6 to 17.4 ms; 2.0 to 2.3 ms alone). The bound is unchanged. Fourth version,
after reviews 6008872330 and 6012650944 and the owner's decision on the third (no vendored patch, no
10-minute exclusive window). `ci/cache.sh` holds the flow (`ci_step_job_in_box`, `ci_step_integration`,
`ci_box_lock`, `ci_box_unlock`): short jobs take `locks/box.lock` shared around their run; `integration`
runs every scenario but the timed one shared (`ci/job.sh integration untimed`, which also compiles),
then takes the box exclusive for the timed scenario alone (`ci/job.sh integration timed`). The test
binary picks its scenarios from `NERVA_INTEGRATION_SCENARIOS` (`src/nerva/integration_scenarios.zig`;
unset runs all; unknown or empty is NRV-E1246); the 50-lookup check moved out of `scenario_cluster` into
its own `scenario_latency`; the two Python tests that follow the executable skip themselves when the
selection holds no untimed scenario (`timed`, `latency`; pinned by `ci/ci_test.sh`). The turnstile (`locks/turnstile.lock`) is held only while waiting. One budget
per call (`CI_BOX_WAIT`, from fixed limits, floor 6480 s: the longest single hold, not a bound on the
queue, since each integration step queued ahead can cost a short job a whole drain; 2 calls = 216
minutes at the very worst),
exclusive run limit `CI_INTEGRATION_RUN_MINUTES` 5. Only flock status 1 is NRV-E1245; other statuses
are NRV-B1204. Long jobs (`fuzz-long`, `vopr-long`, `jepsen`) take no lock. `build.zig` and UPSTREAM.md
are back to main's: nothing vendored differs. `ci/ci_test.sh` tests the flow dynamically with stub jobs
(the timed run never runs under the shared lock; mutants of `ci/cache.sh` and `ci/job.sh` are caught).
Measured here (4 cores, Debug): whole `test:integration`, variable unset, 611 s, every scenario passes;
`NERVA_INTEGRATION_SCENARIOS=timed`: 7 s in all, lookup median 1.4 to 1.5 ms, so the exclusive window is
seconds. Decision O12.
